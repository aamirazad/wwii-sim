General Sheets

| Overview of the History Sim |
| ----- |
| The goal of the history sim is to show how members can apply their knowledge of World War II and to understand the events that caused the war to play out the way it did. Students should always do their research on a country, as historical accuracy and creative planning can give you benefits. Students should also aim to take something away from the sim, and always have fun. During the sim, students are assigned a country and given the statsheet listed below, and are expected to use mod request forms to interact with the sim and other countries in meaningful and historical ways. There are three resource types, steel, oil, and population. For any questions on rules, consult both this document and the public rules sheet. If your questions are still unanswered, you can ask a mod for assistance. |

| Mod Telegraphs |  |
| ----- | :---- |
| General Dispatch (White) | Use this paper for any instances not covered by other sheets, such as spying on other countries, non conference communications, or creating troops. Always record the cost of the operations you take. |
| Research Request (Green) | When doing research to improve your country’s military efforts, select which type of research you are attempting and record the costs. You can submit multiple levels of research at once. |
| Request for Conference (Purple) | In order to meet face to face with another country to discuss plans, you must fill out this sheet by including countries involved and the topic, and a mod must be present during the meeting. |
| Trade Agreement | If you are sending resources to an ally country, record the amount of resources sent, the country it is sent to, and the transport costs. The transport cost is 1 steel and 1 oil for every 4 resources sent. |
| Troop Movement | When setting up for a battle, you may need to relocate troops across the continent or even the ocean. Record the amount of troops, where they came from, where they are heading, the reason, and the resources spent to move them. It costs 1 oil to move a troop. It also costs 1 oil a year to occupy a territory you do not own. |
| Battle Plans (Pink) | If you initiate combat, write down how many troops, where they are attacking, and strategic plans/goals to give your troops the best chance at victory. Detail is important\! |

| Spy Rules |
| ----- |
| Spies can be sent to another country in order to gather information about their military goals. One spy dice costs the same as one infantry dice. You are more likely to succeed in spying against countries you are strong allies with. Be as specific as possible in your goals. |

| Morale |
| ----- |
| Countries will have their morale, or public support, recorded on a public google sheet. Morale will rise in accordance to victories and actions you undertake, including the objectives listed for your country, and will fall as a result of loss or poor management. Morale from 0-10 Possible Civil War or Governmental Overthrow,Morale from 11-20 Widespread Riots,Morale from 21-30 Peaceful Protests Occur,Morale from 31-50 No change,Morale from 51-70 \+1 SP and OP, \+5 PP,Morale from 71-80 \+2 SP and OP, \+5 PP,Morale from 81-90 \+3 SP and OP, \+6 PP,Morale from 91-100 \+1 level of each resource |

Country Specific Sheets: Commonwealth  
(Work with United Kingdom)

| Objective | Rewards |
| :---- | :---- |
| Ship at least half of your OP and PP per level each year to the UK | \+1 OL, \+1 SL, \+1 PL |
| Retain OP and PP equal to amount listed in your level each year | \+3 OL, \+3 SL, \+3 PL |
| Unsuccessful attacks on your territory | \+2 OL, \+2 SL, \+2 PL \+6% per failed attack |
| Japan takes Oceanic islands | OL=1 until reclaimed \-20% Morale until reclaimed |

Objectives Key  
OP=Oil Point                                        OL=Oil Level  
SP=Steel Point                                      SL=Steel Level  
PP=Population Point                             PL=Population Level  
RL=Research Level (can be used in any category)

| Tokens |
| ----- |
| Tokens are used in order to reward historical accuracy at a mod’s discretion. Tokens give a variety of benefits, and can be activated by indicating so on a related mod telegraph. |
| The Commonwealth’s tokens give the following benefit: You can spend a token to gain 25% of your level’s resources instantly. You gain the resources in all three columns of your statsheet. You start with 1 token. |
