\*Another important detail is that our goal is only to simulate the war side of the time period. Some parts of the politics and the social conflicts of the time are not a focus of our simulation, and it is expected that everyone involved will act like adults and be respectful

## RESOURCES OVERVIEW:

 There are three resources. For three of the resources, oil, steel, and population you have a “level” for each. At the start of each year, which will be announced by the moderators, you gain the number of resource points that your statsheet says you gain for that level. You gain levels for doing things that your country would historically have done. For example, if you do something good and gain the support of your people, you may level up your population. Each of the three main resources levels up independently of the other. You also have a starting value for each resource. That is how many resources you start with at the beginning of the game. You start at level 5 for each resource.

## Battles 

The battle system is based on d6s (six-sided dice). In general, a “high roll” is determined to be a roll of 4, 5, or 6\. A “low roll” is a roll of 1, 2, or 3\. Before the dice are thrown, select high or low. The results of the battle. The main way dice are calculated is based on the amount of troops brought. For infantry battles, a die can be bought for 2 population points, 1 oil point, and 1 steel point. 3 naval dice may be bought for 1 population point, 5 steel points, and 3 oil points. Submarines cost 2 population points, 3 steel points, and 3 oil points for two dice. You get some of your population and steel points back depending on what portion of your army in that battle survives. Oil points however, are not replenished.

## Research

Another thing you can spend your points on is research. You may research any path up to 5 times. The price of research is included in the classroom post and is unique to each country. There are 10 different fields to research:   
Land Research

| Research Type | Effect |
| :---- | :---- |
| Tank Warfare | 20% more dice when infantry dice are bought |
| More efficient movement(does not stack) | All oil prices are reduced by 50% for transporting troops on land & occupying territory |
| Improved Weaponry(only one level) | If you pay one steel, you can re-roll all extreme failures (1 or 6), once per battle |

Sea Research

| Naval to Naval battles | 20% more dice when dice are bought |
| :---- | :---- |
| Coastal Defenses | 20% more dice when dice are bought |
| Submarine Warfare | Level 1:Ability to use submarines Levels 2-5: 20% more dice when dice are bought |

Air Research

| Bombing | 20% more dice when dice are bought |
| :---- | :---- |
| Anti Aircraft | 20% more dice when dice are bought |
| Improved Plane weaponry | If you pay one steel, you can re-roll all extreme failures (1 or 6), once per battle |
| Dogfighting | 20% more dice when dice are bought |

Spy Research

| Evade | Add a die to the total dice rolled and require one more dice to be correct to be captured(per level) |
| :---- | :---- |
| Endure | Add a die to the total dice rolled and require one more dice to be correct to gain information from the captured spy (per level) |
| Enlighten | Add a die to the total dice rolled and keep the same number of correct  dice to gain information from the country (per level) |

PRICE TO SEND RESOURCES:  
1 oil point and 1 steel point for every 4 resources you wish to spend (rounded up).

Naval blockade rules (mainly for Germany) \- A blockade may be set up using the normal price for navy, however you only get 1 die instead of three due to your blockade not all being centralized in the same space \- your blockade has a lot of ground to cover and you won’t have all your ships in the same spot when someone attempts to break it. Submarines, as they are far and away the most effective vehicles for surprise attacks and blockades, do not require more resources to make. Blockades cost their price in oil points every year to retain. The moderator will give you orange tokens to represent the price of maintaining your blockade each year.

Occupying a country \- uses the same system as above, but troops are obviously used instead of navy. Use the troop price for dice. The more dice you have in a region to hold it, the less likely you are to lose it in resistance.
